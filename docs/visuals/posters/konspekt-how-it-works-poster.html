konspekt · typed knowledge graph for AI project state

How it works

konspekt keeps the state of an AI project — decisions, context, open questions, provenance — as a portable, human-readable graph. The whole shape of it, on one page.

01 · interact

You and the model

Work happens in conversation between a person and an LLM.

02 · capture

Verbatim, hashed

The exchange is stored word for word, both sides, and addressed by a hash of its bytes.

03 · propose

A typed atom, bound

From the source, the model proposes an entity — concept, finding, artifact, or work node — bound to the graph. No atom floats free: a conversation resolves to entities or is recorded as a deliberate non-binding.

04 · accept

A named human

Nothing counts until a named maintainer accepts it. That is where responsibility attaches.

05 · graph

Into the record

Accepted atoms join one typed edge table, and the authority verbs act on them from there.
