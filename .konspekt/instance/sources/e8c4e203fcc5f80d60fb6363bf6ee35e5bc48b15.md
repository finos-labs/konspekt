foldProposal (implementations/fleet/app/fold.mjs) unconditionally rewrites
entity.md onto canonical with no dedup and no incremental-outbox read.

Observed 2026-10-21 fleet UAT: re-running `run-pass` with an already-folded
proposal still present in its outbox CLOBBERS a blessed atom back to
review: proposed and duplicates its rows.

Workaround in force: consume (git rm) each folded proposals/<id>/ from its
outbox and commit before the next run-pass, so the next fold reads 0. All
three UAT outboxes were emptied this way.

An idempotent/incremental fold is a deferred committer milestone.
