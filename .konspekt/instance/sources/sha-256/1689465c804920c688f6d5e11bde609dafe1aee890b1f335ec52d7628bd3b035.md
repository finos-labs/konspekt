**Claude:**

Two of the five noteworthy kinds should have a status, and the standard already says so. The gap is that the status is optional for those two kinds, and in this instance about half of the constraints were written without one.

[...]

- `NOTEWORTHY_STATUS_BY_KIND` in `lib/conformance.mjs:59` gives status values to two kinds only:
  - `assumption`: `unvalidated | validated | refuted`
  - `constraint`: `active | lifted`
- The checker warns when a `fact`, `statement` or `decision` carries a status (`status-without-vocabulary`). It reports nothing when an `assumption` or `constraint` has no status.

[...]

| kind | files | with status |
|---|---|---|
| fact | 15 | 0 |
| statement | 12 | 0 |
| decision | 84 | 0 |
| assumption | 2 | 2 (`unvalidated`) |
| constraint | 19 | 9 (8 `active`, 1 `lifted`) |

[...]

**My recommendation**
1. **Keep the split by kind.** [...]
2. **Make status required for `assumption` and `constraint`.** [...]
