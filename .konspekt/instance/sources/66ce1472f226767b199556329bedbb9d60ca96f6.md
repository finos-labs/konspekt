## assistant (selection)

The property `nw-fleet-committer-role-split` protects is twofold: the graph is never left partially written, and the accepted commit carries the human `Signed-off-by`. Both survive when (a) the write operations remain deterministic verb implementations with post-write verification, and (b) acceptance is a human-issued verb carried in the conversation. Under those two conditions the committer agent invokes verified operations under human acceptance authority; it does not decide what lands.

... the change is a narrowing of the claim, from "no model process touches the write operation" to "no model decides what is written; every write is a deterministic, verified operation performed under human-issued acceptance."
