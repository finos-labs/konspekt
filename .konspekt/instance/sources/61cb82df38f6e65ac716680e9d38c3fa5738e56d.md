konspekt couples its operating surface to one LLM vendor. The agent-integration
files live under `.claude/` — skills, the conversation-binding gate and its
hooks, the per-session binding markers, and settings — but most of that is not
Claude-specific: a skill is a plain prompt, and the binding and operating
discipline is vendor-independent policy. konspekt's portability goal
(goal-portability) is about carrying a project across platforms, yet today that
portability stops at the data under `.konspekt/instance/` and does not reach the
operating surface, which a second vendor's tooling cannot discover under
`.claude/`.

Decision: move the vendor-neutral parts of `.claude/` to a neutral location (for
example `.konspekt/agents/` or a top-level `agents/`) — the skills, the binding
gate, and the operating hooks — and leave only genuinely Claude-specific wiring
under `.claude/`, as a thin adapter that points Claude Code at the neutral
location. Each additional vendor gets a similarly thin adapter. This makes the
fleet's proposer and committer skills, and the binding discipline, reusable
across tools rather than bound to one.
