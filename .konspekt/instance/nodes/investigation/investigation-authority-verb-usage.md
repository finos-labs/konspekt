# Investigation: Authority verb usage under single-individual authority

In practice this instance exercises only the forward-lifecycle verbs — `accept`
and `resolve` — and almost never the revision verbs (`validate`, `refute`,
`lift`, `abandon`, `pin`). The open question is whether that is a gap or the
expected shape of the single-individual posture: the revision verbs presuppose
claims that are asserted and later contested, or assumptions that drift over a
long horizon, and much change is already carried by `supersedes`. A propose-only
agent plus a hard accept gate means little enters the graph un-adjudicated, so
there is rarely anything settled to later overturn.

Scope to settle first (open): whether `accept` belongs on the deck's Control
Verbs surface at all, given it is a review disposition rather than a status/
standing verb; whether the deck and mental model should separate a hot path
(accept, resolve) from a cold path (the revision verbs); and whether the cold
verbs are standard-level vocabulary that only a multi-author or long-lived
posture exercises. Relates to [[concept-propose-accept-separation]].
