# Noteworthy: Validation experiment has six open design items

Held as one unvalidated item because none is settled. The open decisions:
(1) parallel-groups vs crossover -- lean crossover, not locked; (2) what the
control group gets -- true nothing vs a sham context tool, bearing on the blinding
threat; (3) the single pre-registered primary endpoint -- argued to be
confidently-wrong rate on the start-of-session probe battery; (4) how tightly
"same type of problem" is constrained across participants; (5) duration per arm
and washout length in the crossover; (6) human-subjects overhead -- consent,
dropout, and whether the trial is built to publish (IRB bar). Resolving these is
the next step under `investigation-validation`.
